Notes
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Signatures are the top-K features by cluster-pooled mean cross-classifier
    PERCENTILE RANK importance, weighted by selection frequency across the
    discovery folds. Ranks — not raw SHAP magnitudes — are what make a linear
    model's |coef| and a tree model's mean |SHAP| comparable enough to average
    across classifiers. Raw magnitudes are reported in the |imp| column above
    but are not the ranking key.
  - K = median per-fold signature size from the winner-classifier folds.
  - Consensus choices (signature + classifier identity + hyperparameters) are FROZEN.
  - Classifier weights and fusion coefficients ARE REFIT within each CV fold,
    using only that fold's training data — no test-fold leakage.
  - The consensus signature was chosen with knowledge of all complete-case
    outcomes, so it carries selection optimism that this re-evaluation does not
    remove. Only the weight-estimation and fusion steps are honest here.
  - Report the pooled out-of-fold AUROC with a PATIENT-LEVEL bootstrap interval
    (revision_analyses.py), not the standard deviation of per-fold AUROC.
